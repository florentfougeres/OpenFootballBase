# Changelog

[Français](../fr/changelog.md)

Changes to the website and its tooling, newest first. The data releases (`Data X.Y.Z`, with tag `data-X.Y.Z`) and their notes live in the data repository.

Data releases (`Data X.Y.Z`) are listed in the [data repository's changelog](https://github.com/florentfougeres/OpenFootballBase-Data/blob/main/docs/en/changelog.md).

## Site and tooling

### 2026-11-25
- **Every table in the clean style**: the shared `.tbl` class loses its strong rules (quiet headers, airier rows, edges aligned with the card padding). This covers competition standings, the player's statistics by season, competition and national team, and the tables in the club overview and "This season". The coverage grid (heat map) keeps its grid form.
- **Player page header rebuilt as a single clean card**: club-colour edge and tint, square photo framed on the face, name and club, then the facts value first with the label below (career matches and goals, country, age with birth date, height, number, position, place of birth), with no boxes or rules. The photo credit moves to the foot of the card.
- **Cleaner career list** (like FotMob): a single "Career" card in sections (Professional career, National team, Youth national teams), one row per spell with crest or flag, name, dates, then matches and goals aligned on the right; no more ruled table. The timeline stays an option.
- **Player page in two columns on desktop**: career and national teams on the left (what people come for), this season and the latest matches on the right; the facts grid no longer has an empty cell. On phones the career stays first.
- **No more middle dots "·" in the interface**: lists use commas (title years, scorers, leg scores), qualifiers use parentheses (division, competition, area), and tab titles read "Club 2026/27 – OpenFootballBase".
- **Club page: an "Overview" tab** (inspired by FotMob), the **first tab** and opening view, with the "About" sheet on top. It shows the **last 5 results**, the **next match** (date, time when known, home or away), a seven-row **mini league table** around the club with a link to the full table, the **club's top scorers** (when scorers are recorded) and each **coach's record** (matches, win %, points per match), all computed from the OpenFootball matches (CC0); a block with no data disappears. On phones the next match and the table come first and the latest matches are folded under their five form pills. The "About" sheet (nickname, city, founding, stadium, Transfermarkt/FBref/WorldFootball links, map) is no longer a tab: it sits in the overview as cards (one fact per row). Tabs are Overview, Squad, Movements, Coaches, Honours (short phone labels: Moves, Coaches, Honours).
- **Player page: key facts, this season and latest matches.** A grid under the header gives country, age (with the birth date), height, shirt number and position (the number only when it is the current season's), place of birth. The Career tab opens on **This season** (matches, goals, assists, per-competition detail from the Wikipedia statistics tables) then **Latest matches** (started or sub, score, goals) when OpenFootball line-ups exist for the player.
- **Club page: ergonomics pass** (from a critique, 27/40). On phones the five tabs fit (short labels: Moves, Coaches, About), the season ‹ › arrows are back and each player row is fully tappable (44 px). A season with no squad offers "See the last known squad" and the picker says "No squad" instead of "∅". Shirt numbers in full colour, "—" for an unknown age, the division badge explained on hover, and the browser tab title carries the club name and season.
- **Club page: it opens on the squad.** Tabs are now Squad, Movements, Coaches, Honours, then **About** (the former "Overview", with the map). The current coach shows in the header, and Transfermarkt, FBref and WorldFootball become buttons at the top of "About". The "Build a line-up with this squad" button is removed.
- **Player page: the header says where he plays.** Club (crest, link), position and shirt number from his last listed season, with "Last known club · season" when it is not the current season. The breadcrumb is hidden on phones.
- **Faster start-up.** The 20 views over range-read tables (matches, coaches, stats, honours…) are no longer created at start-up but on the first query that mentions them: creating a view reads the file footer, one network round trip each, which cost about 5 s on GitHub Pages before the home page. The home-page tables and `career` stay immediate.

### 2026-11-24
- **Line-up builder: usability pass** (from a design critique). The tool now opens on **Formation** with a hint on the empty pitch ("Tap a + to pick a player") and an **Auto line-up** button. Choosing a player is a **tray** at the bottom of a phone (the pitch moves up so the chosen position stays visible and highlighted) or a **list in the side panel** on a computer; after a player is placed on an empty position, the next empty one opens by itself. Positions have a **44 px tap area**, and no text on the pitch goes below **8 px** on screen. **Undo / redo** (50 steps), and the line-up is **saved in the browser** per club and season and restored after a reload. The default style comes from the **club**: shirts in its colours (also first in the colour lists), with the fine settings folded under **Customise**. **Photo** and **shirt** are mutually exclusive (choosing photos switches the marker to a disc and disables the shirt; leaving photos brings the previous marker back). On a phone, **PNG image** shows the picture with a **Save to Photos** button (the system share sheet) instead of sending it to Downloads. Sharing uses the phone's share sheet. The club page has a **Build a line-up** button that opens the tool with that squad.
- **A "More" tab replaces "Follows" in the phone's bottom bar.** It opens a page that lists Line-up, Follows, Ballon d'Or, Coverage, About and Settings. The pitch discs of the line-up are smaller.
- **Line-up builder (v1).** A new **Line-up** page (sidebar; on a phone, the new **More** tab) to build a starting eleven. Two sources: **By club** (search a club and a season: the squad is the pool and the club crest is on the pitch) or **All players** (search any player of the database; each result shows his latest club and season). Pick a formation (12, from 4-3-3 to 5-4-1), tap a position and choose a player; "Auto line-up" fills a club squad by position. **Copy link** puts the source, formation and players in the address, so anyone opening it sees the same line-up; **PNG image** exports the pitch with the crest. The side panel has three tabs: **Options** (title, subtitle, switches to show the formation, the club badge and the **nationalities**, goalkeeper at the top), **Formation** and **Style** (what the player discs show: initials, shirt number, a free number, the position or the **photo** from the Wikipedia profile, cropped on the top of the picture where the face usually is). Changing the formation never removes a player (each keeps his line, or takes the nearest free position). Player names sit on a dark plate (with a round flag when nationalities are shown); plates on a row move apart or shrink slightly instead of overlapping or being cut. On a phone the whole tool fits one screen: the pitch takes the available height, the settings open in a sheet from a bottom action bar (Settings, Share, PNG, Clear). The **Style** tab lets you pick the marker (a disc or a **shirt**, with its colour, a pattern — plain, halves, stripes, hoops — a second colour and a colour for the goalkeeper), what it shows (initials, shirt number, free number, position, photo, or **nothing**) and three **sizes** (marker, number, name). Everything goes into the shared link. The PNG export ends with a thin band under the pitch: the site logo and "Built with OpenFootballBase". Formations and the pitch drawing come from the author's Tactica11; no new dependency.

### 2026-11-23
- **The List / Map switch of Explore is compact on a phone**: two small buttons beside the title instead of a full-width bar that overflowed the screen.
- **Season club cards all have the same height** on the competition page: a long name ("Borussia Mönchengladbach") that wraps no longer makes its card taller than the others.
- **The mobile search button is centred in the bottom bar** (it used to stick out above the pill).
- **The same tab bar everywhere.** Club, player, competition (Season / Results / Honours) and Competitions (Clubs / National teams) now use the one `Segmented` component: a full-width bar on a phone on every page (club, player, competition), and the bar scrolls sideways when it does not fit (five tabs on a club).
- **Club coaches completed from the coaches' infoboxes.** Wikidata lists only some head coaches on a club's page (Manchester City showed four, nothing between 2013 and 2016). The Coaches tab now adds the coaches whose Wikipedia infobox ("Teams managed") names the club, assistant posts excluded, for those Wikidata does not already give for that club.
- **The coach page shows the Wikipedia infobox career** (clubs and national teams, assistant roles included, with club crests and national-team flags) instead of only the Wikidata tenures of the clubs we cover; those remain as a fallback.
- **Coach page.** Clicking a coach in the club's Coaches tab opens his page (`#/<lang>/coach/<QID>`): infobox (country flag, tenures, first tenure, birth, Wikipedia), his coaching career club by club, and, when he also played in our data, his playing career with a link to his player page. Birth place and Wikipedia pages of coaches who never played, and the flags, come with the next data release.
- **Coaches tab: the whole list at once, with a flag.** No more "All coaches" button: every tenure is listed. Each coach gets a small flag of his country when the data has it (new `flag` column of the `coach` table, filled by the next data release; until then the list has no flags, and older releases keep working).
- **Coaches get their own tab on the club page** (Overview, Squad, Movements, Coaches, Honours), shown only when the club has coach terms. The Overview now puts the mini-map beside the club details. With five tabs the bar scrolls sideways on a phone and keeps the active tab in view.
- **Mobile search laid out properly.** The results panel used the width of the input only (Cancel took the rest of the row): the field and Cancel now share the first line and the results span the full width underneath; a long placeholder ends with an ellipsis instead of being cut. The desktop palette is unchanged.
- **Mini-map on the club page.** The Overview tab shows the club's crest on its location, at regional scale (never down to a street), under the club's details; only for clubs with a known position. The map is loaded only when it scrolls into view, uses the same light/dark CARTO basemap as the Explore map, and needs Ctrl + scroll (two fingers on a phone) to zoom so it never hijacks page scrolling. MapLibre loading and theme handling now live in `lib/maplib.ts`, shared with the Explore map.
- **Club counts per country only count clubs with a real squad.** The Explore Countries list and the map counted every club that has a `club_season` row, even with no player (Bulgaria showed 53 clubs for 4 squads). They now use the clubs that have players (`app.squadClubs`); the home ranking is fixed in the data release.
- **Home says where the data comes from.** A line under the search box states that the data is open (squads, careers and results from Wikipedia, Wikidata and OpenFootball) with a link to About.
- **Map mode in Explore (Clubs and Countries).** A List / Map switch sits next to the title (`?m=map` in the address). **Countries**: one circle per country with its number of clubs; tap a country (or its circle) to open it, and the United Kingdom offers its four nations. **Clubs**: the 2,460 clubs with a known position, grouped into clusters that open as you zoom in. Zoom goes down to a region (the Paris area fills the screen), never to a street: from regional scale a lone club shows its crest, its name appears on hover or keyboard focus, and a click opens its page; where clubs still overlap at the maximum zoom, a cluster opens as a list. MapLibre GL JS, loaded only when the map is shown, on a light (Positron) or dark (Dark Matter) CARTO basemap following the theme and switching live with it. Clusters are computed in the page (supercluster, one level at a time), which fixes the stacked and flickering clusters of the first version. A lone club's crest is shown whole, on a white tile that never crops round or wide logos. In Countries mode the country shapes (`public/geo/countries.json`, simplified from datasets/geo-countries by `web/scripts/build-countries-geo.py`; Natural Earth, public domain) tint the covered countries under the map labels. Markers are real links and buttons (keyboard and screen reader), the zoom buttons are 44 px, the colours follow the light/dark theme, and the List view stays the accessible alternative. The MapLibre worker is started from a blob (allowed by the site's CSP). **New third party**: the basemap (style, vector tiles, fonts) comes from `basemaps.cartocdn.com`, added to the CSP; CARTO sees the visitor's IP address, only when the map is opened (attribution © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO shown on the map).
- **Ballon d'Or moves to Competitions.** It is content (rankings by edition), not a service page, so it no longer sits next to Coverage and About in the sidebar and the Settings page: it is now a card at the top of the Competitions page, and Competitions stays highlighted while you read it.
- **Home gets to the content.** The big title, the introduction and the figures strip are gone from the top (the page keeps a visually hidden heading for screen readers). You land on a search entry, your follows (with real crests, competition logos and player initials), the featured competitions as chips, then featured clubs and countries as dense rows, two columns on large screens. The database figures move to the bottom of the page.
- **Club page: fewer stacked controls.** The tabs and the season selector now form one sticky bar (under the top bar on mobile, at the top on desktop). **Movements** is now a tab of its own (Overview | Squad | Movements | Honours) instead of a switch inside Squad. The season strip is replaced by a season button (with the division) that opens a list of every season, plus previous/next arrows on larger screens; on mobile, nationality and on-loan filters fold into a Filters sheet with a badge counting active filters.
- **New `Sheet` component**: a bottom sheet on mobile, a centred dialog on large screens (Esc, backdrop or button to close; focus enters, stays inside and returns to the trigger).

### 2026-11-22 (branch `redesign/app-ux`)
- **Settings get their own page.** On mobile they were under the Following tab, which was an odd place for them. A gear icon in the top bar now opens a Settings page (theme, language, Ballon d'Or, coverage, about); the Following tab only lists what you follow. The top bar no longer carries separate theme and language buttons.
- **App-like redesign, mobile and desktop.** *Mobile*: a five-entry bottom bar (Home, Explore, a central Search button, Competitions, Following); search opens full screen with recents and suggestions; a compact top bar with a back button inside child pages; short slide transitions in the direction of navigation (none with reduced motion). *Desktop (from 1024 px)*: a left sidebar (search, navigation, your follows, theme and language) replaces the top bar; search is a centred palette (`/` or Ctrl/⌘ K).
- **Explore**: one page for Clubs, Players and Countries (view in the address, `?v=`), replacing three separate entries.
- **Compact player page on mobile**: the header goes from about 700 px to about 200 px (thumbnail photo beside the name, age and nationality on one line, place and height on a discreet line, "1,217 matches · 948 goals" in place of the two large tiles, Follow and Wikipedia as two 44 px icons). The Career / Stats / Honours tab bar and the first career rows are visible without scrolling, and the bar stays stuck under the top bar to switch views without scrolling back up. The photo credit moves to the bottom of the page on phones; large screens are unchanged.
- **Following**: pin a club, a player or a competition (Follow button on each page). Follows live only in the browser of the device, with no account and nothing sent; they appear at the top of Home, in the sidebar and on a Following page, which also holds the settings (theme, language, Ballon d'Or, coverage, about) that used to be behind "More".
- **Squad side panel (large screens)**: clicking a player in the squad list opens a summary (career totals, last clubs) next to the list, without leaving it; a modified click or a narrower screen still opens the player page.
- **FotMob-inspired density and badges**: club and country lists become dense 48 px rows (crest, name, value on the right, thin dividers) under a sticky country header; standings show the last five results as W/D/L badges (green, grey, red; letter plus colour) from small screens up, and under the team name on mobile; position tags (GK, DF, MF, FW) share one component.
- **Fix: tabs flickering on the player page.** Changing a tab changed the address, which rebuilt the route and reloaded the whole player page: the Honours tab disappeared for a moment (and the page fell back to Career if you were on it). A parameter-only change no longer rebuilds the route, so nothing reloads when switching tabs or views.
- **Calmer Squad tab**: the season strip is one line of round chips (season and division side by side) with small, discreet arrows; Players | Movements, the nationality filter, the on-loan toggle and the list/cards display now sit in a single toolbar; the filtered count and "Reset" move onto the summary line.
- **One tab control**: the club and player tabs, and the Explore views, share a `Segmented` component (arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs).

### 2026-11-22
- **Player page in three tabs**: Career (clubs as a table or timeline, then national-team caps), Stats and Honours, under an unchanged header. A tab with nothing to show does not appear (and the bar disappears when only Career is left); Honours appears once its content is loaded. The tab is in the address (`?tab=`), so it can be shared and the back button restores it; left and right arrows, Home and End move between tabs. ### 2026-11-21
- **Lighter, clearer Squad tab**: three levels clearly separated. The *view* (Players | Movements) and the *display* (list or cards, two icons on the right, players only) fit on one line; the four large figures become one summary line; the *filters* (nationality, on loan) are grouped below (no more position filter: the squad is already grouped by position), and the "11 / 42 players" counter with "Reset" only appears when a filter is active. The first player is visible without scrolling on mobile.
- **Club page in three tabs**: Overview (the club's profile: city, founding, stadium, league, links, coaches, and a shortcut to the season's squad), Squad (with a Players | Movements switch to see arrivals and departures from one season to the next) and Honours. Title chips no longer sit under the club name: honours have their own tab. The tab is in the address (`?tab=`): it can be shared and the browser's back button restores it. Without `?tab=`, the squad opens when the address carries a season (from a table or a season's list) and the overview otherwise (from the club list, search or a player's career). The side column is gone: honours and the profile each have their own tab.
- **Compact club page on mobile**: the header goes from a large tinted block of over 300 px to a card of about 150 px (logo and name side by side, country and division on one line, the club's colour band on a neutral background; the near-identical second name no longer appears). The season strip runs edge to edge, without arrows on touch. The first player is visible without scrolling. Large screens keep their layout; the club tint is lightened there (11%) so the text stays readable.
- **Interface audit, three passes**:
  - *Accessibility*: every page has its own tab title ("Arsenal FC · 2026/27 · OpenFootballBase", the player's name on their page); the light theme's accent is a little darker (`#087245`) so green text on pale green and white text on the accent exceed 4.5:1; field placeholders, the Coverage page heat map (black/white switch point computed for each theme) and active season chips (which had dark text on green because of a global link rule) also meet 4.5:1; "reduce motion" removes smooth scrolling and slides.
  - *Mobile*: the filters of the Players and Coverage pages take half the height (full-width search, season and nationality side by side, position on one line, age and "playing abroad" behind "More filters"; the "squads only" checkbox becomes a toggle chip); header buttons, breadcrumb and home links at 44 px; no functional text under 11 px any more; the player page's career and national-team tables put the years under the club name; club names in the standings wrap onto two lines instead of being cut.
  - *Polish*: no single word on the last line of a paragraph, About page lines capped at 58 characters, Ballon d'Or years no longer cut off with "…".
- **Compact filters on the Competitions page (mobile)**: on phones, the Clubs / National teams switch is full width and the confederations fit on a single scrolling line (they took three lines): the list of competitions starts about 200 px higher. Nothing changes on large screens.
- **No more broken images**: a player photo (or a competition logo) deleted from Wikimedia since the data was published no longer shows a broken image with its caption: the photo disappears from the player page, the logo gives way to a trophy or the country flag (as club logos already do with the crest). The data is fixed at the source by `enrich --what imagecheck` (data repository).
- **Pill-shaped mobile tab bar**: on phones, the bottom navigation is now a pill set on a dock (a solid band at the bottom of the screen: the page stops above it, nothing shows through), in the manner of iOS tab bars. A capsule slides under the active tab, including to "More" while its panel is open. Same five entries and labels still always visible; "reduce motion" removes the slide.

### 2026-11-18
- **Two repositories**: the website stays here; the data, the pipeline and the releases moved to [OpenFootballBase-Data](https://github.com/florentfougeres/OpenFootballBase-Data). A release published there starts the site deployment (`notify-site.yml`, token `SITE_DEPLOY_TOKEN`); `deploy.yml` takes the release tag as input; `npm run data` downloads the data for development. Plan: [migration-two-repos.md](migration-two-repos.md).
- **Competitions page**: a Clubs view (by confederation, then a "World" section for intercontinental cups, no longer mixed with UEFA) and a National teams view (finals, qualifiers, friendlies), with icons and the competitions' Wikipedia logos; competitions known only from the match source sit in their continent.
- **Results tab** on every competition page: computed standings with form, matches by matchday with goalscorers; honours tab aligned with the club honours panel. Weekly script: `matches` (results, then current-season completion from Wikipedia) and `enrich --what leaguelogos` after it.

### 2026-11-16
- **Consistency audit** (`audit`): checks the whole database before every publication, run by the weekly script (a blocking problem stops it). Statistics: goal counts that are impossible (more than 3 per match and at least 10, typos in Wikipedia such as "11 matches, 100 goals") are now left empty instead of displayed; tables whose season cell carries a stray `!colspan` no longer shift the columns.
- **Weekly refresh** (`scripts/weekly_refresh.sh`, [weekly-refresh.md](https://github.com/florentfougeres/OpenFootballBase-Data/blob/main/docs/en/weekly-refresh.md)): script for a server cron that re-reads the players still active (career infobox and statistics tables), exports and publishes a release. New option `stats --active`.

### 2026-11-13
- **Security and speed**: Content-Security-Policy; the DuckDB engine is verified by a SHA-384 integrity hash and its Parquet extension is hosted with the site (see [security.md](security.md), [website.md](website.md)); `restore-db` verifies every file's SHA-256; deployment downloads the release that triggered it; GitHub Actions pinned by SHA; Dependabot, secret scanning and push protection enabled.
- **Releases renamed** `Data X.Y.Z` with tag `data-X.Y.Z` (they used to be `data-v1` … `data-v13` with names like "Data v1.10.0 (date)").
- **Bilingual data dictionary** (`SCHEMA.md` in English, `SCHEMA.fr.md` in French) and bilingual documentation under `docs/fr/` and `docs/en/`; bilingual README.
- **Competitions page**: uniform grid grouped by confederation; continental cups appear under their confederation.
- **Site**: Players section (filters, CSV download), league trends dashboard, career timeline, club movements between seasons, unified club + squad page, coverage heat map.
- **Tooling**: `openfootball restore-db`, `openfootball continental`, `--fresh` mode and `tools/refresh.sh --fresh` for the transfer window.
